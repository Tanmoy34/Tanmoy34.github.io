Project Overview

Rocket Booster is a 3D physics-based navigation game built in Unity using C#. You play as a rocket starting on a launch pad — pressing the throttle to fire the engines and steering left or right to dodge obstacles in your path. Touch an obstacle and the rocket blasts apart; reach the landing pad safely and you progress to the next level.

Built solo as part of my Udemy Unity learning journey, this project focuses on cementing core Unity fundamentals: physics-driven movement, camera work, audio and particle effects, and scene management.

Key Features

  • Physics-driven rocket movement using Rigidbody forces and torque
  • Throttle & Steering — thrust with Space Bar, steer with A / D
  • Obstacles — collide with one and the rocket explodes
  • Landing Pad — successful landing advances the player to the next level
  • Cinematic camera tracking that follows the rocket through each level
  • Thrust, explosion, and landing effects built with Unity's particle and audio systems

Core Systems

  • Unity New Input System — modern input reading for throttle and steering
  • Physics & Rigidbody Forces — AddForce and AddTorque for realistic rocket movement and rotation
  • Cinemachine — dynamic camera follow and framing
  • Audio System — sound effects for thrust, collisions, and landing
  • Particle System — thrust flames, explosion effects, and landing effects
  • Scene Travel — loading the next level or restarting on success/failure
  • Prefabs — reusable rocket, obstacle, and effect prefabs for fast level building

What I Learned

  • Organizing code into clean, reusable structures with Namespaces & Classes
  • Handling keyboard input for throttle and steering with Unity's New Input System
  • Driving realistic rocket movement and rotation with physics forces and torque
  • Setting up cinematic camera follow and framing with Cinemachine
  • Adding sound effects and particle effects to enhance gameplay feedback
  • Managing scene transitions between levels
  • Building and using Prefabs for efficient, reusable level design
